The idea behind Digital Vote was inspired by a real-life situation that made me think about the accessibility of election-related services. My brother had to travel all the way from Bangalore to cast his vote, spending approximately ₹2,000–₹3,000 on travel alone. This made me realise that many people living away from their hometowns, along with elderly citizens and individuals with mobility difficulties, may face similar challenges.

I wanted to explore how technology could make election-related information and services more accessible, convenient and organised. Digital Vote was created with the vision of bringing essential election services together on a single digital platform, including candidate information, polling station details, application management, complaint handling and election administration.

Through this project, I aim to demonstrate how digital technology can support greater accessibility, transparency and efficiency in election management.

Digital Vote is an independent educational demonstration project. It does not conduct real elections or provide online ballot casting.
